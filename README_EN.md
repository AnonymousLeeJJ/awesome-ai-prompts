## Why this list?

Most prompt collections on the internet are one-liners like "You are an expert, please help me X". This list only includes **structured, copy-ready prompts verified in real-world scenarios**. Every prompt follows three standards:

- ✅ **Structured**: role + rules + steps + output format for stable results
- ✅ **Detailed**: includes criteria, edge cases, and explicit constraints
- ✅ **Templated**: replace the `【fill in】` placeholders and go
